      📐 Official Camera Setup & Distance Rules

      Side-On Distance

      5.0 to 7.0 meters perpendicular to crease. Keeps entire body visible from gather to release.

      Tripod Height

      1.0 to 1.2 meters (waist height). Prevents perspective foreshortening distortion.

      Recommended FPS

      60 fps or 120 fps slow-motion recommended for high arm rotation speeds.

      ✍️ Feedback on Coaching Observation

      Help refine the model. If an observation was inaccurate or affected by camera angle, you can dismiss it or provide a correction note.

      📐 Camera Setup Guidance

      Side-On (Perpendicular to Pitch) — Best for Elbows Place phone 5-7 meters from popping crease at waist height (1.0m - 1.2m). Ensure full body is visible from run-up stride to release.
      Front-On (Behind Stumps) — Best for Alignment Position 6-8 meters behind target stumps at chest height (1.2m - 1.5m). Ideal for detecting head fall-away and trunk tilt.
